About KrishiLoop

Bridging the Agricultural Residue Divide

Transforming an ecological challenge into a transparent, income-generating circular supply chain.

⚠️

The Ground Problem

Every year, Indian fields produce over 500 million tonnes of crop residue. Due to the lack of organized local collection, transparent grading, and guaranteed offtake, farmers are forced to spend scarce capital clearing fields or burn remaining stubble.

  • • Clearing Burden: Clearing banana stalks costs farmers ₹12,000–₹15,000 per hectare.
  • • Arbitrary Pricing: Middlemen exploit farmers with arbitrary discounts and no standardized scales.
  • • Air Quality: Massive open-air burning degrades soil and causes seasonal respiratory smog crises.
🌱

The KrishiLoop Circular Model

KrishiLoop builds digitized village-level collection centers where farmers drop off residues, witness transparent electronic weighing, receive automated quality grade certification, and lock in guaranteed industrial buyer matching.

  • ✓ Digital Calibration: ESP32-linked strain gauge scales ensure tamper-proof measurement to 0.1 kg accuracy.
  • ✓ Parametric Grading: Clear benchmarks for harvest freshness, moisture, and contamination eliminate guesswork.
  • ✓ Guaranteed Offtake: Supply partnerships with paper mills, fiber extractors, and packaging plants.
Process Workflow

How KrishiLoop Works

A seamless 4-step journey connecting farms to circular industry consumers.

01

Intake & Digital Weighing

Farmer brings crop residue to the local KrishiLoop village hub. Calibrated load cells register gross and tare weights instantly with digital logging.

02

Quality & Moisture Check

Standardized checks measure days since harvest, moisture content, and foreign matter. The system automatically computes Grade A, B, or C.

03

Buyer Match & QR Tagging

The platform matches the batch with suitable industry buyers (paper mills, fiber units, packaging). A unique QR code is printed for dispatch tracking.

04

Direct Payout to Farmer

Upon receipt confirmation by the buyer, payment is automatically disbursed to the farmer's bank account or UPI within 48 hours without intermediary cuts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with KrishiLoop.

Every KrishiLoop village hub is equipped with an IoT-enabled load cell digital platform running calibrated firmware. Residue weight, moisture levels, harvest freshness, and contamination percentages are entered into the terminal, automatically generating an objective Grade A, B, or C with tamper-proof QR tracking.
Farmers receive payment directly into their bank accounts or UPI within 24 to 48 hours of dispatch verification, eliminating the weeks-long delays and arbitrary deductions typical of traditional informal middlemen.
We currently process Banana Pseudostem (fresh harvested), Sugarcane Bagasse, Cotton Stalks, and Dry Cereal/Paddy Straw across regional collection clusters in Maharashtra and adjoining agricultural belts.
Buyers access our digital marketplace to set minimum intake grade criteria, contract required monthly tonnages, and view batch-specific lab certificates and dispatch logs with complete farm-to-factory traceability.
